The Sport

About Horseshoe Pitching

Horseshoe pitching is one of America's oldest backyard sports — a game of skill, precision, and consistency. Players take turns pitching horseshoes at a stake set in the ground 40 feet away (27 feet for women and juniors), trying to ring the stake or land as close as possible.

Basic Rules & Shot Values

  • Ringer (3 pts) — The shoe encircles the stake. A straight line touching both ends of the shoe must clear the stake.
  • Leaner (2 pts) — The shoe leans against the stake. Counts as a scoring shoe.
  • Scorer (1 pt) — The shoe lands within 6 inches of the stake but does not lean.
  • No Score (0 pts) — All other shoes that do not meet scoring criteria.
  • Cancellation Scoring — Only the player with the closest shoe or ringer scores. Equal values cancel each other out.
  • All-Counting (Count-All) — Both players score the total point value of all their scoring shoes each end.
  • Standard game plays to 40 points, or best of a set number of ends.
🔗 U.S. Horseshoe Pitchers Association (NHPA) 📋 Official Rules & Regulations
